# DogBooker marketplace roadmap

Updated 7 November 2026. This replaces proposal-only status for the local technical slice; it does not authorize a paid pilot, public deployment or legal assumptions. The earlier local roadmap was reviewed and its independent-product boundary retained.

## Delivered local slice

Separate app/database/auth, multiple dogs per owner, synthetic signup, sitter profiles and explicit available dates, deterministic explained matching, requested/confirmed/declined/cancelled bookings, immutable price/care snapshots, tenant-protected agenda, PostgreSQL daily inventory and global dog-overlap constraints, concurrency/migration tests and Swedish responsive UI. No standalone migration is needed. See [implementation report](marketplace/IMPLEMENTATION_REPORT.md).

## Future capabilities investigated, not implemented

**Stripe Connect.** Direct, destination, and separate-charge/transfer models put different responsibilities on the platform and sitter. Select the contractual seller and refund/dispute liability first; do not assume a fee model or call delayed transfers escrow. Engineering dependencies: integer immutable quotes, hosted onboarding, capability state, signed durable webhook inbox, idempotent charge/refund/transfer operations, double-entry ledger, reconciliation and support controls. A redirect cannot establish paid status. Review settlement/hold/country rules with Stripe before implementing real fund movement. [Stripe charge models](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/charges)

**Identity verification.** Verified email, age/eligibility, fraud screening and legally supported identity checks are separate from sitter competence, permits and suitability. Store provider references/status rather than raw identity documents where possible. Add expiry/review/appeal flows and role-limited operator access. Provider selection and local legal basis are unresolved; there are no verification badges in this demo.

**Animal welfare, insurance and incidents.** Swedish guidance says some dog businesses or larger-scale keeping need county administrative board permission. Sitter capacity in software is not proof of a permit. Establish premises/competence/resident-animal requirements with the relevant authority. Bind appropriate custody/care, veterinary, third-party and property coverage before any coverage claim. Define emergency contacts, veterinary authorization, escape/injury response, evidence handling and staffed escalation with an insurer/broker and operator. [Jordbruksverket](https://jordbruksverket.se/djur/hundar-katter-och-smadjur/hundar/sa-skoter-du-din-hund)

**Verified reviews and messaging.** Limit reviews to a defined completed-stay eligibility rule with one review per booking, provenance, moderation and appeals; do not suppress adverse reports. Reviews require lifecycle/audit data first. Messaging requires booking-party scope, abuse reporting, attachment validation/EXIF handling, redaction, retention, notifications and narrowly audited support access. Never expose another owner's dog schedule by default.

**Fraud prevention.** Prioritize account verification, listing moderation, booking/hold limits, rate limiting, duplicate/idempotent writes, suspicious self-booking detection and manual review. Payment fraud and account takeover need provider signals and incident tooling. Prefer explainable flags and appeal paths to irreversible algorithmic bans.

**GDPR and retention.** Map controller/processor roles, purpose/lawful basis, data minimization, vendor agreements, transfers and deletion/export obligations. Determine DPIA need. Set retention for search locations, care snapshots, identity checks, chats, ledger and audit data with explicit exceptions; add access logs and breach handling. Dog/location data can identify a household. Current snapshot access restriction is not a complete retention program. [IMY principles](https://www.imy.se/verksamhet/dataskydd/det-har-galler-enligt-gdpr/grundlaggande-principer/)

**Swedish/EU commercial rules.** Counsel must assess consumer/trader disclosure, withdrawal and cancellation terms, ranking/review transparency, accessibility, platform-work/business-user terms and local business obligations. Assess DSA classification and applicable exemptions before implementing trader traceability, notices, explanations and appeals. Assess DAC7 registration, seller due diligence and personal-service reporting, plus VAT/invoicing and accounting; a payment provider is not assumed to discharge platform obligations. These are launch acceptance criteria, not a claim that every rule applies identically to this project. [DSA regulation](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32022R2065), [Skatteverket platform reporting](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretag/drivaforetag/startaochregistrera/digitalaplattformarlamnauppgifteromsaljareuthyrareochersattningar.4.7c708f0e16bed42cd0555d5.html)

**Geographic expansion.** Before adding markets, introduce sitter-local timezone/country/currency rather than assuming Stockholm/SEK, verified location/geocoding and PostGIS geographic filtering with pagination. Review permits, insurance, contracting, tax/reporting, support language, DST and payment support for each jurisdiction. A Finnish operating entity does not automatically make Swedish boarding rules irrelevant, or vice versa.

**Natural-language matching.** First gather structured quality and outcome evidence. Let language models propose owner-confirmed requirements or explain existing scored factors; never infer missing critical safety facts or override hard restrictions. Use minimized inputs, offline evaluation, versioned outputs, privacy review and adversarial prompts before a reranker experiment.

**Meta Muse connector.** Current official guidelines describe complete task flows, read/write/sensitive classifications, scoped permissions, privacy/security requirements, business verification and review. A booking integration should carry search → full quote/terms → authorized request/checkout → status/cancel/refund where supported, preserve uncertain outcomes and avoid duplicates. Start with a sandbox scope and explicit confirmation for consequential writes. OAuth/revocation, stable idempotency and booking/payment state must exist first. No connector has been submitted, approved or connected, and no undocumented SDK/payment contract is assumed. [Muse connector guidelines](https://platform.muse.ai/docs), [connector terms](https://platform.muse.ai/terms)

## Remaining explicit decisions

Operating entity and first locality; boarding/charging semantics; contractual seller and payment liability; sitter screening/permit evidence; insurance and emergency coverage; household sharing; retention/deletion; hold deadlines; support staffing; provider contracts. The synthetic prototype can be independently reviewed without making these commercial decisions. They gate launch, not further local testing.
